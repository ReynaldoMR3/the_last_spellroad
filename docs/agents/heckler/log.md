## 2026-07-21 — HP/Hexcoin mechanic bundle critique (dispatched by Ana)

Critiqued the new HP pool (100, no in-combat regen, reset per wave/checkpoint), the three enemy archetypes (Melee 7/Ranged 4/Debuffer speed-or-Manaregen drain), and the Hexcoin-gated phase-transition HP recovery (15%/phase-break, fee = 50% of [BASIS FLAGGED]) against `hp-template.md` and `hexcoin-template.md`.

- **Systems designer:** BLOCKING — paying Hexcoin to recover up to 15%/phase-break (stacking across multiple phase-breaks in one boss fight) lets currency substitute for tactical play, undermining the stated "wins through positioning, not reflexes" pillar; also flagged the Mana-regen drain floor (2/sec) as unreachable dead code given the hard 2-application stacking cap (5 − 1.5×2 = 2 exactly).
- **Narrative critic:** MAJOR — the mid-fight pay-to-heal fee and the Debuffer archetype both ship with zero fictional framing (no lore hook, no explanation of what spending coin to heal mid-boss-fight means diegetically), reading as a bare monetization-style toggle bolted onto tactical combat.
- **Player psychologist:** BLOCKING — the recovery fee scales with Hexcoin *earned this expedition*, so players who performed well can easily afford it while players actually spiraling toward death (low HP, likely low Hexcoin) can least afford the mechanic meant to save them — inverting the stated purpose of preventing an "unavoidable death spiral."
- **Feasibility lead:** BLOCKING — the fee's basis (expedition-scoped vs. lifetime Hexcoin) is explicitly unresolved in both templates ("Do not implement against either reading until confirmed"), so this bundle cannot actually be built as-is despite being dispatched to close the top BLOCKING finding.
- **Adversarial QA:** MAJOR — no cap on total phase-recovery purchases within a single multi-phase boss fight (a 5-phase boss could recover up to ~60% of the pool across 4 breaks), and "computed fresh at the moment of that phase-break" leaves undefined whether mid-fight kills (minions/adds) feed the "earned this expedition" total, opening a possible self-funding fee loophole.
- **Business analyst:** BLOCKING — two Hexcoin sinks (the existing flat 100-fee spell-choice and the new 50%-of-earnings recovery fee) now draw on the same expedition-scoped pool whose reachability/farmability is *already* an open, escalated disagreement per `hexcoin-template.md`; the economy cannot be tuned or evaluated until that's resolved, and there's no stated cap preventing Hexcoin-rich players from systematically buying their way around the death-trigger Mastery-loss penalty across a whole run.

## 2026-07-21 — Re-critique of the finalized bundle: five decisions resolved, Pato's templates, Warden's validated boss composition (design/numeric layer only, engine deferred)

Re-ran the same six personas against the same bundle now that the developer has resolved all five items flagged above (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-21-death-recovery-fee-decisions.md`), Pato has folded them into `hp-template.md`/`hexcoin-template.md` in final form, Warden has produced and Pato has independently gate-checked (verdict PASS) the vertical slice's first real boss composition (3 phases / 2 phase-breaks, 51 HP competent / 79 HP careless, 1 purchasable recovery), and the GDD carries all five decisions natively. Scope: design/content/numeric layer only — Loomwright's engine implementation is developer-deferred to a future planning cycle and is not reviewed here. Did not take Ana's or Pato's word that anything was fixed; checked each finding against the actual current field/rule text and, where relevant, against Pato's independent recomputation of Warden's numbers.

- **Systems designer:** MINOR (down from BLOCKING) — the core finding is resolved: the cap formula (`phase-breaks − 1`, hard-ceiling 3) plus Pato's 33%-of-competent-play-threat-budget money ceiling (`hp-template.md`, "Money ceiling") is the actual skill-over-money guarantee, and it isn't just asserted — Pato independently re-derived it against Warden's real boss (`pato/log.md`, entry (5)): 15 HP / 51 HP = 29.4% of budget, under the 33% ceiling with a real (if thin) 3.6-point margin, so currency recovers a documented minority of a well-played fight, not a majority. Left unresolved and un-mentioned by any of the five decisions: the Mana-regen-drain floor "2 Mana/sec regardless of stack count" is still dead code — the hard 2-application cap already produces exactly 5 − 1.5×2 = 2, so the floor clause can never actually bind before the cap does. This was bundled into the original BLOCKING verdict but was never in scope of the five-decision spec (which only touches the recovery-fee mechanic), so it's still sitting there unaddressed. Harmless (it can't cause an out-of-spec value), but it's redundant spec language nobody has cleaned up — downgraded to MINOR/cosmetic rather than reissued as BLOCKING.
- **Narrative critic:** MAJOR, unchanged — confirmed independently (not on Ana's say-so) that this is genuinely still open, not quietly fixed: `docs/agents/lorena/log.md` contains zero entries beyond "context store established," and the decision record itself defers this explicitly ("Lorena — narrative framing for the fee and the Debuffer archetype (still MAJOR, not blocking), once the mechanic is numerically stable enough to be worth narrating"). The fee and the Debuffer archetype still ship with no fictional framing whatsoever. Correctly sequenced (the mechanic needed to be numerically stable first), but "sequenced" isn't "resolved" — flagging so it doesn't fall off the list once the numeric bundle ships.
- **Player psychologist:** CLEAR (BLOCKING resolved) — the specific inversion (fee scaling to exactly match whatever a player currently has, so a thriving player always affords it while a spiraling player with little Hexcoin affords proportionally little) is gone: the fee is now a flat, fixed 30 Hexcoin regardless of performance (`hp-template.md`, "Fee — Pato's numeric call"; `hexcoin-template.md` Fee 2). Because the price no longer tracks the player's own balance, a struggling and a thriving player face the identical number, which is what the developer asked for. New/residual, not a re-issue of the original bug: a flat fee replaces a graduated inversion with a hard affordability cliff — a player with 29 Hexcoin gets zero recovery, identical to a player with 0, while a player with 30+ gets full access regardless of surplus. Not flagging this as a fresh BLOCKING finding because the template already discloses and accepts exactly this tradeoff in its own words ("A player who has coasted or died a lot... genuinely might not be able to afford it... That gap is the intended tension, not a flaw") — but noting it so "resolved" isn't misread as "no player ever gets shut out," which isn't true and was never claimed.
- **Feasibility lead:** CLEAR (BLOCKING resolved) — the fee's basis, explicitly unresolved before ("Do not implement against either reading until confirmed"), is now unconditionally settled: expedition-scoped, resetting at every checkpoint (`hp-template.md` "Basis — resolved, expedition-scoped"; `hexcoin-template.md` "Basis, resolved"; GDD "Phase-Transition Recovery," Basis bullet). All three sources agree verbatim on the same reading — this is buildable as specified, whenever Loomwright's engine work is actually scheduled.
- **Adversarial QA:** MAJOR (new finding; original MAJOR resolved) — the original two findings are both closed: the cap-formula + money-ceiling pair bounds total recovery per fight (confirmed against Warden's actual composition, not just the rule text), and the mid-fight-kill freeze (`hp-template.md` "Mid-fight-kill freeze," decision 4) closes the farm-to-afford loophole by snapshotting the eligible balance at fight-start. New hole the resolution itself opens: the whole flat-30-Hexcoin fee is priced against an assumption that per-expedition income is a hard, one-time bound ("forward-only... bounds per-expedition Hexcoin income to a predictable number," `hp-template.md`'s Fee reasoning) — but "forward-only" is only ever defined against *cleared* road segments (GDD, Gameplay Loop: "once a road segment is cleared, the mage cannot backtrack into it"). Nothing in the bundle says whether a boss segment counts as "cleared" before it's beaten, or what happens to the waves leading up to it on a retry: death sends the mage back to "the last road-segment checkpoint" (`hp-template.md` Death trigger; GDD Death And Mastery Loss), and if that checkpoint sits before the pre-boss waves rather than immediately at the boss door, dying and retrying could re-earn Hexcoin from the same not-yet-cleared waves each attempt — quietly un-bounding the exact income figure (~20-70 Hexcoin) Pato used to justify the flat-30 number. This doesn't invalidate Warden's already-validated composition, but it's a real gap in the economic model the five decisions never addressed, grounded in a specific unstated interaction (checkpoint placement relative to boss-retry) rather than a vague "farming feels possible" vibe.
- **Business analyst:** CLEAR (BLOCKING resolved) — forward-only (GDD, Gameplay Loop) directly closes the reachability/farmability dispute this finding depended on, and the two-fees-one-pool tension is now explicitly owned as intended design rather than left as an unresolved risk (`hexcoin-template.md`, "Resolved 2026-07-21 — the two-fees-one-pool tension": Fee 2 affordable against the ~20-70 estimate most of the time, Fee 1 deliberately not, spending on one is a real tradeoff against the other, and Pato explicitly declines to re-price Fee 1 to compensate). The "buying around the Mastery-loss penalty across a whole run" half of the original finding was always slightly overstated on inspection — Fee 1 never removes the mastery-tier loss, it only makes the loss non-random, so there was nothing to newly bound there. One dependency worth naming: this whole resolution's economic soundness (both the reachability closure and the flat-30 pricing) inherits the same open question the Adversarial QA persona raised above about checkpoint/retry income — if that gap turns out to unbound income, the "bounded, predictable" framing this business-analyst clearance rests on would need re-examination. Not re-issuing the original finding, but the two personas are pointing at the same seam.

**Overall:** four of six personas clear the original bundle outright (Feasibility Lead, Business Analyst) or resolve to a materially better state with only a disclosed, non-blocking residual (Player Psychologist, Systems Designer). One re-affirms an already-known, already-deferred gap rather than discovering a new one (Narrative Critic — MAJOR, sequencing not a defect). One surfaces a genuinely new, specific hole the resolution itself introduces (Adversarial QA — MAJOR: checkpoint/retry income bounding is unstated and the whole flat-fee price depends on it holding). No BLOCKING findings remain against this bundle at the design/numeric/content layer. The design/numeric/content layer (five decisions, both templates, one validated boss composition) is clean enough to mark `shipped-and-validated` for that layer specifically; the two open MAJORs (narrative framing, checkpoint/retry income bound) should stay tracked as separate, named follow-ups rather than silently closed, and engine implementation remains explicitly out of this critique's scope per the developer's own deferral.
