# Level 1 Opening Experience Brief

This is the stable cross-agent reference for the approved Level 1 art-and-music direction. Load only the sections owned by the dispatched role. The full rationale and prototype boundaries live in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-07-opening-art-music-prototypes-design.md`.

## Shared target — Ana routes

The Opening Experience is the player's first minute in Level 1. It must lead with magical excitement while preserving tactical readability and leaving room for later terrain and music to become stranger and more melancholic.

The art direction is **Runes Awake** at **Arcane Momentum** intensity. Familiar stone, forest, and warm natural light ground the scene. Saturated cyan, gold, violet, and ember-orange magic supplies the excitement.

## Visual direction — Tilesmith owns

- Keep stone and forest recognizable, restrained, and readable at the real 960×540 canvas scale.
- Concentrate saturation and animation in runes, spell trails, impacts, pickups, and the reactive shrine.
- Preserve clear silhouettes and targeting geometry; decoration must not resemble hazards or obscure enemies.
- Use the existing CC0-first sourcing order. Prefer a hybrid treatment: CC0 terrain foundation plus deterministic original glyphs and spell effects.
- Later levels may cool and desaturate the terrain, thin the vegetation, and make the road's geometry more imposing. Level 1 should not spend that melancholy immediately.

## Narrative and musical direction — Lorena briefs, Composer realizes

- The first emotional promise is magical capability and forward momentum, not loneliness.
- The opening motif should remain transformable: later arrangements can slow, thin, darken, or move it toward minor harmony without losing its identity.
- Favor fast plucked strings, celesta or bright bells, hand percussion, and an ascending magical motif.
- Avoid epic-orchestral bombast, dense synthetic spellstorm textures, and a triumphant cadence that makes the road feel conquered.
- Composer produces deterministic notation and a browser-ready render from Lorena's approved tempo, harmony, instrumentation, duration, and loop brief.

## Interaction baseline — Loomwright owns

- Use the current production Level 1 geometry and gameplay timing.
- Treat issue #68's resolved road-feel decision as fixed: discoverable side-pockets plus a reactive shrine, with ambient dressing underneath.
- Every audition treatment uses identical movement, enemy silhouettes, spell timing, and one showcase cast. Presentation is the variable.

## Validation — Heckler challenges, developer approves

- A new player should identify the mage, enemies, walkable road, pickups, shrine, and spell area immediately.
- Magic should feel exciting within 30 seconds without making the terrain visually noisy.
- Music and effects must loop/mix cleanly and must not mask gameplay-relevant SFX.
- The winning treatment must remain viable on the low-spec browser target and within the Docker-only production workflow.
- No sourced or generated asset is production-approved without provenance and human license review.
