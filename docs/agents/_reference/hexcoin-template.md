# Hexcoin Template (Pato's authority)

Source of truth: `docs/game/the-last-spellroad-design.md`, "Hexcoin" and "Phase-Transition Recovery".

- Earn rate: **1 Hexcoin per kill**, flat across enemy types.
- Persists through death like every other permanent-progression element — never lost.
- **Fee 1: 100 Hexcoin** lets the player choose which equipped spell takes the Mastery-tier loss on death, instead of a random roll.
- **Fee 2 (resolved 2026-07-21, restore amount revised 2026-07-23): flat 30 Hexcoin**, same price every time regardless of how the run is going, lets the mage buy one Phase-Transition Partial HP Recovery (10% pool restore, revised down from 15% so the money ceiling is reachable across the full competent-play budget range — see `hp-template.md`) at a boss/trial phase-break — see `hp-template.md`, "Phase-Transition Partial HP Recovery" for the full rule, the cap formula, the money ceiling, and Pato's fee-sizing reasoning. This replaces the earlier "50% of [BASIS FLAGGED]" model entirely — there is no percentage-of-earnings component left in either template.

**Basis, resolved:** both fees above draw from the same **expedition-scoped** Hexcoin sub-total — earned since the current expedition/road-segment began, resetting to 0 at every checkpoint. Not the lifetime balance. (Developer decisions 2 and 3, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-21-death-recovery-fee-decisions.md`.) This was Pato's original "on this road" reading and the flag against it is now closed, not just recommended.

**Mid-fight-kill freeze (Fee 2 only):** the balance eligible for Fee 2 is frozen at boss-fight start. Hexcoin earned mid-fight (adds, summons killed during the boss encounter) still banks toward the expedition total, but cannot fund that same fight's own recovery fee — see `hp-template.md` for the full rule and the reason (closing the farm-to-afford loophole). Fee 1 is not fight-scoped, so this freeze does not apply to it.

**Reachability/farmability, resolved:** the Spellroad is strictly forward-only — no backtracking into a cleared road segment (GDD, Gameplay Loop). This closes the 2026-07-21 review-board disagreement (`gdd-review-kit/reviews/2026-07-21/SYNTHESIS.md`) about whether the Hexcoin economy is reachable at all or trivially farmable: it is neither. Per-expedition income is now a bounded, predictable number instead of an open question — which is also the fact that let Pato pick Fee 2's flat 30-Hexcoin amount instead of guessing against an unbounded economy (see `hp-template.md` for the income estimate behind that number).

**Resolved 2026-07-21 — the two-fees-one-pool tension (previously an open flag):** Fee 1 (100 Hexcoin) and Fee 2 (flat 30 Hexcoin) draw on the same bounded, expedition-scoped pool. Now that backtracking is settled forward-only and both fees are flat numbers, this is not a problem to fix — it's the intended shape of the economy:

- Plausible pre-boss income for a reasonably active player, vertical-slice scope, is roughly 20-70 Hexcoin (see `hp-template.md`'s Fee reasoning for the wave/kill-count estimate behind this). Against that range, Fee 2 alone (30 Hexcoin) is affordable for most of it; Fee 1 alone (100 Hexcoin) is not, most of the time — the GDD already frames the 100-Hexcoin fee as "deliberately steep... not a formality," i.e. pitched above a typical expedition's income by design, not comfortably within it.
- A mage who pays Fee 2 during a boss fight banks that much less toward Fee 1 later (or toward the next expedition's Fee 2). That is a real, felt tradeoff, not a design defect — it is the same "pay because it's worth it, or decline and come back better prepared" choice the developer asked for, now extended across two fees competing for one pool instead of one fee against an unlimited one.
- Pato is not resolving this by re-pricing Fee 1, and is not trying to guarantee both fees are simultaneously affordable within one expedition — that would defeat the tradeoff. If a future playtest shows Fee 1 is effectively *unreachable* within a normal expedition's income (as opposed to merely steep), that's a developer call to revisit Fee 1's own number, not something Pato quietly compensates for by repricing Fee 2.

**Resolved 2026-08-01 (backlog item 0.2, developer decision) — checkpoint/retry income-bounding, the gap the 2026-07-21 Adversarial QA finding flagged, is now closed rather than left as an accepted risk.** A death's checkpoint respawn places the mage at the start of the *current* road segment (level), and waves replayed on that retry re-award Hexcoin normally — this is exactly the scenario that finding worried would break the bounded-income assumption.

**Corrected same day, per Heckler's critique of the first implementation:** the first pass zeroed the whole balance to 0 on every death (`resetExpedition()`), which Heckler's arithmetic showed was a BLOCKING regression, not a fix — Level 4's own kill budget (25) is *below* Fee 2's 30-Hexcoin cost, and forward-only means a death in or after Level 4 permanently locked the balance under 30 with no way to earn it back, for the rest of that expedition. **Fixed with a floor mechanism instead of a hard reset:** `HexcoinSystem.markLevelStart()` records the balance the instant a new level number is first reached; a death now calls `rollbackToLevelStart()`, which undoes only the failed attempt's own partial gains, never dropping below what was already banked from levels genuinely cleared. This still bounds what one retry can add (capped by that level's own kill count, the original intent), while never creating the Level-4-and-later lockout the flat reset did. See `docs/game/the-last-spellroad-design.md`, Gameplay Loop, "Checkpoint granularity, resolved 2026-08-01" for the GDD-level statement of this.

## Side-Pocket Lore Encounter Reward (resolved 2026-08-09)

- Reward: **2 Hexcoin**, fixed, for each of the four Level 1–4 Side-Pocket Lore Encounters.
- Award exactly once, when that pocket's persistent lore flag changes from undiscovered to discovered. A skipped pocket awards nothing and remains available on a later expedition; an already-discovered pocket never awards again.
- The award is permanent progression: commit it to the retry floor when collected so `rollbackToLevelStart()` cannot remove the 2 Hexcoin while leaving the one-time lore flag consumed. This is an exception only to failed-attempt rollback, not to either fee's expedition-scoped spending basis.
- If collected before a boss/trial starts, the award is part of Fee 2's fight-start frozen balance. It can never be earned during that fight, so it cannot bypass the mid-fight-kill freeze.

**Economy check against all shipped first-expedition enemies:** Levels 1–4 contain 16/19/19/20 enemies (**74**) and the Invigilator's three phases contain 5/7/7 (**19**), for **93 kill income**. Four pockets add 8, making the maximum unspent first-expedition balance **101**. Unspent balances are **18 / 39 / 60 / 82** after the four pockets, then **87** after boss phase 1, **94** after phase 2, and **101** after phase 3. Fee 1 first becomes reachable on the **sixth kill of phase 3**: 94 + 6 = 100. That does weaken Fee 1 relative to the no-pocket 93 ceiling, but acceptably: the choice remains unavailable until the final two enemies, and only if the mage found all four optional pockets and declined Fee 2. "Deliberately steep" means an exhaustive, unspent clear can barely reach it, not that the first expedition must make it impossible.

**Fee 2 / boss cap check:** the boss-start frozen balance is 82, so the flat 30-Hexcoin recovery is affordable. The shipped Invigilator has 3 phases / 2 phase-breaks, therefore its cap is `min(2 − 1, 2) = 1` recovery, not 2. If bought, the first-expedition ending balance is 101 − 30 = **71**, so Fee 1 and Fee 2 cannot both be exercised on that clear. Mid-fight kills do not alter Fee 2 eligibility because its snapshot remains 82.

**Candidate boundary check (1–5 per pocket):** full-expedition balances without a recovery are **97 / 101 / 105 / 109 / 113**; after the Invigilator's one permitted 30-Hexcoin recovery they are **67 / 71 / 75 / 79 / 83**. One per pocket never reaches Fee 1. Two reaches it at boss phase 3 kill 6. Three reaches it at phase 3 kill 2. Four reaches it at phase 2 kill 5. Five reaches it at phase 2 kill 1. Values 3–5 pull the steep fee too far forward; 2 is the smallest value that creates a meaningful threshold while keeping it at the expedition's extreme end. No candidate permits Fee 1 after buying the recovery.

**Why not 1 Hexcoin:** it is safe but insufficient as an optional-detour reward. Four pockets add only 4 Hexcoin and leave the first expedition capped at 97. Cumulative balances through the boss are 78 at boss start, 83 after phase 1, 90 after phase 2, and 97 after phase 3. It changes neither the first 30-Hexcoin reachability point nor the one-recovery capacity, and never activates the 100-Hexcoin choice. One pocket is also worth only one ordinary kill. Two is sufficient because the complete discovery set creates one narrow, late-game decision boundary without weakening either fee earlier in the expedition.
